Why Relvia

Not five apps stitched together.

Most platforms make you choose: watch here, create there, connect somewhere else. Relvia doesn't split those apart.

1

Made and watched in the same place

A film gets collaborated on in Studios, then streams right here — no export, no separate platform, no waiting for a different app to catch up.

2

Creators actually get paid

RelvBits let anyone tip a streamer or a filmmaker directly, with real payouts through Stripe Connect — not vague "someday" monetization.

3

One identity across the ecosystem

Post in RelviaCreators, direct message, follow people — and it's the same you whether they found you through Studios, Music, or Live.

4

Built in the open, still moving fast

This isn't a five-year-old platform coasting on features from 2020. Every division here is actively being built and shipped right now.
