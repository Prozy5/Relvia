About Relvia

Built for people who make things.

Relvia Entertainment is one platform for film, music, live streaming and the creators behind them. Make it, share it, get paid for it, without stitching together five apps.

Our mission

One place to make, show and earn.

Creators shouldn't need a dozen tools to finish one project. Relvia brings the making, the audience and the money into one platform, so the work stays yours.

Relvia is built independently, and it's still growing. Some divisions are in beta, and we say so on each page when that's the case.

Make it

Studios and Music give you the tools to build, cut, release and collaborate.

Show it

Live and Creators put your work in front of people who want to see it.

Earn from it

Tips, memberships and payouts are built in, not bolted on.
