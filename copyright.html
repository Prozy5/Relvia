Copyright takedown requests

If someone has copied your work and posted it on a Relvia site without your permission, send us a notice here. We review each notice and remove content that is infringing.

Where to send notices

Notices can be sent through the form below. Relvia's designated agent for copyright notices is listed here once registered.

Before you file

  1. Identify the copyrighted work you own, and the link to the original if it is online.
  2. Identify the exact link where the copy appears on Relvia.
  3. Tell us who you are and how to reach you.

Filing a false notice can expose you to liability. Only file if you own the work or are authorized to act for the owner.

Notice form

I have a good-faith belief that the use of this work is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
I state that the information in this notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that I am the owner or am authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
