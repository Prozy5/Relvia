Put your brand on the runway

Showcasing independent brands, designers & labels. Get a page with your lookbook, your shop link, and followers who want to see your next drop.

Start your application Browse brands

Apply

Tell us about your brand, add your shop link, and upload a logo. Takes about ten minutes.

Review

The Relvia team reviews every application. We may ask for more detail. You will get a message on Relvia either way.

Sign

Read and e-sign the RelviaFashion Brand Agreement. It sets out what you own, what Relvia can show, and what happens if you leave.

Publish

Once you are approved and have signed, add your lookbook of photos and short videos, and go live.

What you get

  • A brand page with your story, tagline, logo, and cover photo.
  • A lookbook of up to 60 photos and short videos, each with a caption and a link to buy that piece.
  • A direct link to your shop on your page.
  • Followers who get your new looks.
  • A chance to be featured on RelviaFashion.

Who can apply

  • You own the brand, or you are authorized to represent it.
  • You sell real products, and your shop link works.
  • Everything you upload is yours or licensed to you.
  • You are at least 18, or have a parent or guardian applying with you.
  • You are comfortable with our content rules: no counterfeit goods, no sexual content, no hate.

Your rights and the agreement

You keep ownership of your designs, photos, videos, names, and logos. Relvia gets a limited license to show your work on RelviaFashion while your page is listed. We do not claim ownership of your work.

Relvia can remove content that breaks the rules or gets a valid infringement complaint. You can remove your content or close your page at any time.

The full agreement is shown when you apply. You read it, then sign. Relvia only has obligations to a brand that has signed, and your lookbook opens after you sign.
