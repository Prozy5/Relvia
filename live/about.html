About

The live stage of
Relvia Entertainment

Relvia Live is where creators broadcast in real time — conversations, performances, behind-the-scenes moments, and everything in between. It's built as its own space within the wider Relvia Entertainment ecosystem, alongside Relvia Studios, Relvia Music, and Relvia Social.

Go Live

Broadcast straight from your browser — no separate software required. Pick a category, hit Go Live, and you're on air.

Categories

Streams are organized into categories curated by Relvia's owners, so viewers can find what they're looking for quickly.

Real-Time Chat

Every stream has live chat, so viewers and creators can talk while the broadcast is happening.

Independent Access

Relvia Live uses its own sign-in, separate from Relvia Social — so moderation on one never affects your standing on the other.

Common Questions

Do I need a separate account from Relvia Social?

Yes. Relvia Live is intentionally kept on its own sign-in so that moderation decisions on Live and Social stay independent of each other.

What do I need to go live?

Just a browser with camera and microphone access. No external streaming software is required.

Is there a way to monetize streams?

Creator monetization is on the roadmap. Nothing is live yet — this page will be updated when it launches.
